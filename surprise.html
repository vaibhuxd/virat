Every Little Reason

Why I Love You, Aakanksha

Out of a million things I could never fit on a single screen, here are the little truths my heart comes back to every single day.

01
“How you treat me — like I matter, like I'm safe with you.” The gentlest place my heart has ever known.
02
“You bring out my inner child — carefree, silly, unguarded.” Around you, I forget to be "grown up" and just be.
03
“Har choti choti baat batana — knowing you'll listen, every single time.” Telling you every little thing, no matter how small.
04
“Mere saath rona — crying with me, not just for me.” Feeling what I feel and carrying it with me instead of watching from the side.
05
“The way you remember things I forget about myself.” And how one random text from you fixes my whole day, even when it's been a mess.
06
“Tumhara mazak udana, phir khud hi so jaana.” Teasing me and then falling asleep mid-conversation like nothing happened.
07
“Teri hasi, duniya ki sabse pyari aawaz.” Your laugh is the best sound in the world, and I'd do a lot of dumb things just to hear it again.
08
“With you, I don't have to perform. I can just exist.” And somehow, that's the most peaceful I ever feel. That's how I know this is real.

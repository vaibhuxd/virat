For My Aakanksha

To the girl who treats my heart so gently,

I just love how you treat me — like I matter, like I'm safe with you.

You bring out my inner child. Around you, I act like a kid again — carefree, silly, unguarded. You make me forget to be "grown up" and just be.

Har choti choti baat batana — telling you every little thing, no matter how small. Knowing you'll listen, every single time.

Mere saath rona — crying with me, not just for me. Feeling what I feel, carrying it with me instead of watching from the side.

The way you remember things I forget about myself. The way a random text from you fixes my whole day, even when it's been a mess.

Tumhara mazak udana, phir khud hi so jaana — teasing me and then falling asleep mid-conversation like nothing happened. I love that too.

Teri hasi, duniya ki sabse pyari aawaz — your laugh is the best sound in the world, and I'd do a lot of dumb things just to hear it again.

With you, I don't have to perform. I can just exist. And somehow, that's the most peaceful I ever feel.

And stuff. The things I can't even put into words. The things that don't need a reason — they're just you, and us.

That's how I know this is real.

— Yours, Virat
